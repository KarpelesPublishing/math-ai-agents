# Chapter 4: Stairs

## Height of a staircase

A staircase with $n$ equal steps, each of rise $r$, climbs a height

\[
h = n r
\tag{4.1}
\]

## Walking the stairs one step at a time

Each step adds the same rise, so the height after the third step is three rises.

## What this chapter does not settle

The rule assumes every step has the same rise; a staircase with uneven steps needs the sum of its rises instead.
